# Freight Rate Calculator — Know your costs

## Treatment
- Format: **9:16**, exactly **30 seconds**, silent-readable with one instrumental bed; no narration.
- Promise: Real carrier costs give owner-operators, dispatchers and freight brokers a stronger basis for rate conversations.
- Identity: Existing calculator logo; navy #263449, amber #df872e, ivory #fbf8f0, sage #8aa38e. Manrope display, DM Sans body, DM Mono figures.
- Hero: A large cost-per-mile figure that becomes a shipment quote and then an actual margin. The road centerline is a persistent graphic carrier, not literal routing data.
- Motion: Masked kinetic type; cost-line assembly; perspective scale reveals; road-line match cuts and split-field transitions. Adjacent chapters change field, framing and action.
- Arc: Immediate trucking hook; reveal the cost calculation; let the target rate read; show actual results; connect the audiences; distinct ivory brand finish.
- Payoff: “Know your costs. Quote with confidence.” No launch date, customer endorsement, app availability claim or profit guarantee.
- Assets: Existing SVG logo copied unchanged. One generated conceptual highway truck photograph for shots 1 and 5; no actual carrier affiliation. All displayed financial figures are illustrative.
